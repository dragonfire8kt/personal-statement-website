Why do you want to study this course or subject?

I was introduced to Computing at age 11 in school and immediately found interest in programming. This appeal to me was so strong that I began to teach myself aspects of Python – the language taught – into the GCSE curriculum, despite being two years away from this level. The Head of Computing at my school sent a physical letter to my parents to commend my enthusiasm, and I am still proud to have received this acknowledgement. Since then, I have come to understand that Computer Science is much more than writing code, and these realisations have only deepened my curiosity for this discipline.

I now see that I adore this subject because I enjoy the challenge of logical and mathematical problems and am thoroughly enthused with considering unique yet effective solutions. I love that Computer Science allows me to combine the creative and scientific skills I find enthralling, and I think that this interest makes me a suitable student for the subject.

In lessons, I find myself constantly wanting to dig deeper into the content and understand the “why” that isn’t mentioned at this level. Studying Computing beyond A-level in a research-powered environment will allow me to satisfy the complex depth of understanding I desire and prepare me for a job in this competitive field.
