Why do you want to study this course or subject?

Computer Science isn’t just programming; it’s a combination of scientific and creative disciplines that I find enthralling. Its scope brings challenges, such as the need to regularly update skills to keep pace with its rapid advancement, but my enthusiasm for the subject makes this ongoing learning exciting.

Within my studies, I am motivated to dig deeper into content, and I often conduct further research to explore the underlying “how” and “why”: for example, investigating how AI affects SDLCs and why user interface design drives adoption. Studying Computing beyond A-level in a research-based environment will satisfy my curiosity and prepare me for a career in this field, where I hope to contribute to positive change through innovation.
