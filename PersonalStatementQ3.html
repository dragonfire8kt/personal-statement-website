What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?

This year I completed the NCSC’s CyberFirst Advanced Course at UWE. This was an excellent introduction to higher education, developing my cybersecurity skills and problem-solving within a team. Effective problem-solving requires patience and precision, which I applied when assembling a PC, reinforcing curriculum knowledge through practice. These skills further progressed when programming an Arduino microcontroller, introducing me to C++.

To evolve my professional insight, I spent a week working in Digital Services at a local university. I gained customer service experience that strengthened stakeholder communication in my Computing project. I also saw how AI can support application development, and working with web technologies inspired me to build my own Personal Statement website, available at www.addis.family/~Kaitlin (password 7532).

I keep updated on subject advancements using sources such as lectures, videos and literature. A notable recent experience was watching 3Blue1Brown’s “Compression is Intelligence” series, which I discovered after a taster lecture compelled me to investigate the topic “What is Information?”. These videos explain the principles of Information Theory, leading me to draw stronger links between Maths, Computing and their real-world applications, such as machine learning. Such experiences strengthen my interest in Computer Science, improve my understanding of its mathematical foundations, and drive me to continued subject exploration.
