How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?

For my Computing project I am developing a real-time simulation of airport runway traffic. I have gained knowledge of documentation and requirements-gathering whilst improving programming skills taught in lessons, such as using data structures and iteration. My NEA also provides the opportunity to research beyond the specification; I have, for example, explored using Monte Carlo simulations to model uncertainty. My dedication to Computing theory and practice was honoured when I received my school’s Computer Science award.

My EPQ extended my studies; I explored if gamifying online revision tools could improve GCSE students’ results. My research increased my interest in gamification and data science – through secondary research I uncovered intriguing connections between Computer Science and Psychology, whilst my primary study introduced me to data analysis. I feel the experience was valuable preparation for higher education, especially when developing my skills in presentation and independent learning.

As a CyberFirst Student Ambassador and school Computer Science Prefect I am improving my leadership and confidence whilst enhancing subject proficiency by mentoring, preparing assemblies, and representing the subject. My Graphic Communication studies engage my creativity, prompting me to communicate ideas clearly and create original work. I bring these strengths to Computing by employing user-centred design to create intuitive user experiences. Mathematics tests my logic and abstract thinking in ways easily applicable to Computer Science - I enjoy independently working through challenges and identifying links between the subjects, such as through the PageRank Algorithm.
